These articles are random and unrelated, but they both had crazy headlines or images that made me laugh. I probably won't create a post for stuff like this very often, but you never know... It can be pretty [weird out there](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fQVUlokZvQk&list=PLxkf1-YjHZ7WyGm63fQzwKytuYB1TXe68)...

The first one was about a raccoon that trashed a liquor store. That's pretty funny, right? The title was enough to pull me in. It was titled, [Tipsy Raccoon Wreaks Havoc In Virginia Liquor Store](https://ijr.com/article/tipsy-raccoon-wreaks-havoc-in-virginia-liquor-store) and I am glad it did because the pictures provided in the news story are the main thing that cracked me up and I had to save for all time.

![Tipsy Raccoon](tipsy-raccoon.avif)

Look at that thing! The store aisle would have been a funny place to see it, but sprawled out between a trashcan and a toilet? That's too much! That's genius!

The second article had an equally great title, [Out-of-control robot karate kicks a child in the stomach while performing martial arts](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-15876455/Out-control-robot-karate-kicks-child-stomach-performing-martial-arts.html) and the image attached to it was perfectly timed.

![Robot kicks boy](robot-kicks-boy.avif)

That picture was taken at exactly the right time. The video (in my opinion) makes it slightly less funny, because the boy probably should not have been standing so far into the robot's area and I *do* feel bad for the boy. But... the picture taken at the moment of the strike is just too funny 🤣.
