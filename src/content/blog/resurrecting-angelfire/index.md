A brother from the household got in touch and reminded me of the website I made in college, about twenty-two years ago.

It was called [In His Image](https://ihi-angelfire.therobhenry.com/), and it lived on [Angelfire](https://www.angelfire.com/). I built it in Macromedia Dreamweaver, back when a site was a pile of HTML and CSS files you saved and uploaded. Angelfire shut the old sites down years ago, so this one went down with them.

I had kept a backup. The copy I still have says it was last updated in July 2004. I put that backup back up at [ihi-angelfire.therobhenry.com](https://ihi-angelfire.therobhenry.com/).

It is a crappy site. I am not fixing it up. Opening those files again was the point. The markup is from another era, and it brought back what it felt like to build a page in Dreamweaver when that was the whole job.

Worth keeping for the memory.
