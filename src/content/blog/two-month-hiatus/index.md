I said in [Hello again](/blog/hello-again/) that I couldn't promise this place wouldn't go stale. I kept that promise.

The last post went up in early August. Then a couple of months passed. Nothing fell over — the old posts kept serving — I just didn't sit down and write anything new. Work, other projects, the usual drift.

I wasn't  completely "out of the game". During my hiatus, I worked on a wiring project for my brother-in-law and to help diagram it, I created [Wire-It](https://wire.therobhenry.com/). It's an unfinished but still helpful tool like [tldraw](https://www.tldraw.com/) but for wiring. I also setup a [Vilros Raspberry Pi 5 Retro Gaming Kit](https://vilros.com/products/vilros-raspberry-p-5-retro-gaming-kit?variant=41054667898974) with all my games that I own and backed up with a [Retrode](https://www.retrode.com/en/) years ago and have been enjoying family time destroying my kids in the two best video games ever made, [MarioKart 64](https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Kart_64) and [WCW/nWo Revenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WCW/nWo_Revenge). I had help setting it up (while learning how) using [Hermes](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/) with my new [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/) account.

I'm still not turning this into a posting schedule. The plan will stay the same: short notes when I have something to say, and no pressure to manufacture content. This one is only here to mark the gap and pick the thread back up.

If you stopped by and found nothing new, that's why. Hello again.
