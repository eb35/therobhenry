I have always been fascinated with *building* things. Whether it was Legos or Erector sets when I was younger or websites as a teenager and now, I have always appreciated starting with raw materials and ending up with a finished product. I didn't have the benefit of learning when I was younger, but living on a farm, I have learned how to swing a hammer or two.

I don't seem to find the time to work on small projects, but I can still watch videos or find resources on the web of other people making things. It is not an extensive list of projects, but I had this LifeHacker article saved for a long time. It is titled, [The Best Free Plans for Your DIY Backyard Projects](https://lifehacker.com/home/best-free-plans-for-diy-backyard-projects), and although I might challenge them on their claim of them being the "best", I did find some good ones in the list.

I have not started any of these, but if I ever need to, I will probably check them out to get some ideas starting to flow. Feel free to do the same.
