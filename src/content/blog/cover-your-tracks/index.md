I'm always interested in web technology and it's never boring. We all know that we're being tracked when we browse, so we take precautions. But by the time we learn *what* those precautions **should** be, they've already moved onto something else.

I saw this [LifeHacker article](https://lifehacker.com/tech/how-to-stop-browser-fingerprinting) show up in my RSS feed and had to read it. You can do the same, but my favorite part was about the [Cover Your Tracks](https://coveryourtracks.eff.org/) tool provided by the [Electronic Frontier Foundation](https://www.eff.org/).

You can test how well your browser protects you and it explains exactly what it's testing. If you do test it yourself, (spoiler alert) the [Brave](https://brave.com/) browser kicked Chrome and Firefox's *tuchuses*.
